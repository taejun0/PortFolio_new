The tool-generated PNGs were encoded as AVIF and WebP with the project's existing Sharp dependency. This was format/quality optimization; character extraction and transparency were produced by the image tool. The sprite alpha is preserved. The background AVIFs are roughly 30–33% smaller than the WebP fallbacks; the browser selects one format, and the decoder waits on that same selected image without downloading another format. Browser layers use a normalized 1600 × 1000 desktop or 1000 × 1600 portrait coordinate system. Chair registration offsets are corrected in `Character.tsx`.

## Limitations

The three upper-body poses are painted key poses blended by opacity, with separate wrist motion while typing and subtle breathing. Since the 2026-10-30 motion revision, foreground chair and lower body use a fixed mask from the typing frame, while preparation, hold and return are scheduled separately. They are not a fully drawn frame-by-frame animation. The curtain effect is a slight light wash over the painting, not cloth simulation. Object names remain registration hooks; navigation and object interactions are outside this iteration.
